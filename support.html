Help

Straight answers for the things people ask us about the app.

Pro did not unlock after I paid.
On the Pro screen, tap Restore while signed in to the Apple ID that paid. Still nothing? Send us the order date.
How do I stop being charged?
The Pro screen has a Manage subscription button that jumps to Apple's list. Or go through iPhone Settings, your name, Subscriptions, Lume. Delete-the-app is not a cancel.
What stays free?
The reference book and every marking search, with or without Pro. Pro is for the photo readings.
It named the wrong watch.
Lume lists candidates with percentages on purpose. Add the text printed on the dial or case back in the marking sheet, shoot straight on with the crown at three, and avoid glare on the crystal.
Can I remove a watch from my collection?
Swipe it left in the Owned or Wanted list, or open it and use the delete action. Its photo file goes with it.
How do I erase everything?
Delete Lume from the iPhone. Collection, photos and reading history are local, so that clears them. We hold no copy.
Does it work with no signal?
The book and your collection do. A photo reading needs the internet.
Is the value a real offer?
No. It is an estimated band from the in-app reference, nobody is buying at that price.
