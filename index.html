Know the watch on your wrist.

Lume reads a photo of a watch dial or case back, lines it up against a reference book of 113 models, and gives you honest candidates with a value range, not a single confident name.

PartWhat it does
BenchTake or choose a picture, add markings you can read, get a ranked shortlist
Reference bookAll 113 entries and the marking search are free to open, even without Pro, and they work offline
ValueAn estimated price band per condition, shown as a range
CollectionWatches you own or want, with what you paid, service dates and days worn
Lume ProOptional subscription for unlimited photo readings

Where to go next

Help has answers on restoring and cancelling. Data shows what stays on your phone and the single thing that leaves it. Terms covers billing and the limits of an estimate.

Heads up Estimates are not appraisals. A dealer holding the watch will always beat a photograph.
