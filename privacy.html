Data

What Lume touches, what it leaves alone, and the one network trip a photo makes.

What we touch

  • The photo you submit for a reading
  • Your purchase receipt, via Apple's StoreKit

What we never touch

  • Your location (the app does not ask for it)
  • Contacts, microphone, health data
  • Your collection, notes and prices paid
  • Advertising or analytics SDKs

The photo trip

A reading sends one JPEG over HTTPS to a relay function we operate on Google Cloud. The relay passes it on to OpenAI's image model along with a request for likely watch references, and returns the result. Nothing is saved by the relay; its logs hold failure codes only. While OpenAI processes the image, its API terms apply.

What stays at home

The collection (owned and wanted watches, notes, price paid, acquisition and service dates, wear days), the reading history, and the photo files are kept in a database and folder inside Lume's sandbox. Whether they ride along in an iPhone backup depends on your own backup settings.

Payments

Lume talks to the App Store directly through StoreKit. Apple holds the payment details; we see only whether a plan is active. There is no Lume account and no email collected in the app.

Deleting

Remove one watch by swiping it away or using delete on its page; the photo is removed with it. Remove everything by deleting the app. If you have a question we cannot answer here, write to nastya.shchaveleva@internet.ru.

Kids and changes

Lume is not aimed at children under 13. Material changes will move the date in the page footer.
